# Research Interests
 * Agentic retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) Reasoning
 * Agentic RAG safety (knowledge poisoning) and privacy (knowledge extraction)
 * Neural-symbolic agentic intelligence
 * Social network simulation and analysis
